Amazon Strategy Presentation

By Adrian, Aniss, Andres, Hamish, Naomi, Peter, Sam

Differentiating Factors

  • E-Commerce Dominance: Amazon operates only online, unlike Walmart.
  • Product Diversity: Amazon hosts premium and basic goods via third-party sellers.
  • Delivery & Distribution: 40k+ trucks, 1k+ fulfillment centers.
  • Inventory: Random stow + robotics reduce cycle time.

Market Positioning

  • Target: Prime members, urban professionals, tech-savvy, international buyers.
  • Access: Online-first with hybrid channels (e.g., Whole Foods, lockers).
  • Urban Logistics: Optimized for dense suburban delivery.

Grocery & Fulfillment Strategy

  • Amazon Fresh: Delivery-focused, optimized with HelloFresh partnerships.
  • Whole Foods: Premium in-store experience for wealthy customers.
  • Cold Chain: Regional artisan products with fast delivery logistics.

Strategic Recommendations

  • Spin off Whole Foods & Amazon Fresh to operate independently with Amazon investment.
  • Focus geographically on urban & suburban hubs, avoid Walmart-dominated rural areas.
  • Increase value through Prime bundles, AI personalization, and last-mile innovation.
  • Retain profitable customers by reducing over-generous shipping incentives.

Q & A: Common Questions

Q: Why does Amazon focus on urban and suburban markets?

A: Urban and suburban zones allow faster delivery, better labor access, and a higher density of Prime members.

Q: How does Amazon's delivery network compare to Walmart's?

A: Amazon operates 40k+ semi-trucks, 30k vans, and 110+ aircraft—far more extensive than Walmart’s fleet.

Q: What makes Amazon Fresh different from Whole Foods?

A: Amazon Fresh is primarily online with meal kit integration, while Whole Foods is a premium in-store brand.

Q: Why is spinning off Whole Foods suggested?

A: To reduce overhead, increase grocery profitability, and allow focused strategy under Amazon’s umbrella.

Q: What role do third-party sellers play?

A: They provide product variety, reduce inventory risks for Amazon, and generate high-margin marketplace fees.
